The Story

Back to play

Through survival, discovery, and years of experimenting (latterly shrouded in doubt), I have arrived, with some serenity, at the coast of light play.

I have always been creative in class. I enjoy the randomness of language learning and have long used random generators. I love students making and saying sentences of their own, with a little help from a prompt.

Most of the ideas here have been gathered over my twenty years of teaching. Some name their source; others are simply activities teachers have been doing for years.

Each page has been built with the help of Claude, who has finally enabled me to create what was in my head.

For more of what’s happening on the coast of light, follow the blog or find me on Instagram. Either is a good place to get in touch, send feedback or report a problem.
