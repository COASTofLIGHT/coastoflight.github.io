Opinions, likes & dislikes

Tap the statement for a new one to react to. Use the language below to ask, answer, agree, disagree and personalise.

  • Asking for opinion

    • What's your opinion on…?
    • What do you think about…?
    • How do you feel about…?
    • Would you agree with that?

  • Opinion

    • In my opinion…
    • I think / reckon…
    • From my point of view…

  • Likes / dislikes

    • I'm into…
    • I'm a keen / avid (surfer)
    • I'm keen on / fond of (surfing)
    • I'm a fan of…

  • Agreeing

    • You're absolutely right.
    • I feel the same.
    • I couldn't agree more.
    • I think so too / Totally.

  • Disagreeing

    • I don't think so…
    • I take your point but…
    • Hhhmmm, I'm not so sure…
    • I see what you mean but…

  • Personalising

    • Speaking from personal experience,…
    • For me personally,…
    • I'd prefer … because …
